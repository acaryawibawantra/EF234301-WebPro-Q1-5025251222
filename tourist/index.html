Tourist Places

Places worth visiting

A simple guide to a few places in Denpasar that both visitors and locals keep coming back to.

Sanur Beach

Sanur, South Denpasar

A calm beach on the east side of the city, known for its sunrise and long beachfront walking path. In the morning you can see traditional jukung fishing boats on the water.

Bajra Sandhi Monument

Renon, Denpasar

A monument in the middle of Puputan Margarana park that remembers the Balinese struggle against Dutch colonial rule. There is a museum inside, and the field around it is popular for jogging.

Puputan Badung Square

City Center, Denpasar

A large open field in the heart of the city, next to what was once the palace of the Badung kingdom. Today it is a public gathering place, busiest in the late afternoon.

Bali Museum

City Center, Denpasar

Bali's main museum, just east of Puputan Square. Its buildings use traditional Balinese architecture, and the collection covers the island's history, art, and culture.

Jagatnatha Temple

City Center, Denpasar

A state temple near Puputan Square, dedicated to Sanghyang Widi. Its tall padmasana shrine is one of the recognizable landmarks downtown.

Pasar Badung

City Center, Denpasar

Bali's largest traditional market, on the bank of the Badung River. Traders set up long before sunrise, selling fruit, vegetables, spices, and offerings. Come early to see it at its busiest.
