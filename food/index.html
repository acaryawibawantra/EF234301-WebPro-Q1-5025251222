Local Food

Food from Bali

These are some of the dishes you will find in warungs and food stalls around Denpasar.

Babi Guling

Roast pig rubbed with turmeric and Balinese spices, cooked slowly on a spit over fire. It is one of the island's most famous dishes, usually served with rice, lawar, and sambal.

Ayam Betutu

Chicken coated in a rich spice paste called base genep, wrapped in banana leaf, and slow-cooked until tender. It is one of Bali's classic ceremonial dishes.

Lawar

A side dish of grated coconut, long beans, minced meat, and a generous amount of spices mixed together. It often accompanies babi guling and celebration meals.

Sate Lilit

Minced chicken, fish, or pork mixed with spices, then wrapped around a lemongrass stalk or bamboo stick and grilled over charcoal.

Nasi Campur Bali

Nasi campur means "mixed rice": a plate of rice with small portions of whatever the kitchen offers that day, like shredded chicken, sate lilit, lawar, vegetables, and sambal.

Tipat Cantok

Compressed rice cake (tipat) cut into pieces, mixed with boiled long beans, bean sprouts, and vegetables, then topped with spicy peanut sauce. A common warung snack across Bali.
